# Comparison 4 — pristine upstream `1c1a330` vs current HEAD `e28a5e3`

Full re-measurement, one commit later than [compares/3.md](3.md): the seed-10
UB diagnosis and `temp` defense-in-depth fix (`e28a5e3`,
[MIGRATION.md](../../MIGRATION.md#confirmed--the-seed-10-divergence-mechanism-and-a-separate-defense-in-depth-fix)).
See [4-protocol.md](4-protocol.md) for every command behind every number
below, including the two additions this session made to the standing
protocol (the tightened seed-10 boundary probe, and a UBSan check of the
`swapChains` mechanism itself).

Measured 2026-08-30, same machine as comparisons 2-3: gcc 13.3.0 / clang
18.1.3 / Ubuntu 24.04 / x86-64, Intel i7-12650H (16 threads), all binaries
`-O3 -DNDEBUG`.

## Headline: the fix changed nothing, confirmed at the one point that mattered most

**Every value in this comparison matches compares/2.md and compares/3.md
exactly** — fold, the 16-seed sweep, the crash-rate map (survivor-for-survivor),
NS, checkpoint format, every tools/ finding. That's expected: pristine never
changes, and the one commit since comparison 3 was designed, and independently
verified last session, to be observationally inert.

**What's new is a direct check of that inertness claim, at the single most
sensitive point this whole investigation has ever found.** MIGRATION.md's
"CONFIRMED" section pinned pristine's seed-10 divergence to a single step —
clean at 22,090, diverges at 22,095. This comparison probed exactly that
boundary on current HEAD, post-fix: it stays at `-11.9092` on both sides of
it, same as every other step tested. If the `temp` fix had done *anything*
observable, this is where it would have shown up first. It didn't.

**The `swapChains` mechanism itself was re-confirmed, not just cited.** Last
session's MIGRATION.md write-up recorded a UBSan finding
(`main.c:237`, index 10 out of bounds) as the confirmed cause of the
divergence, fixed already by `7e2192d`, not by anything in the `temp` commit.
This comparison rebuilt both binaries under `-fsanitize=address,undefined
-fno-sanitize-recover=all` from scratch and reproduced it again: pristine
still fails at the identical line, current HEAD is still clean. This wasn't
in comparisons 2 or 3's protocol — it's a standing, re-checkable claim now,
and it held up.

## Conclusion

1. **Four independent measurements now agree on every shared number** —
   fold, the 16-seed sweep, the crash-rate map, NS, checkpoint format, and
   every tools/ finding, across compares/1.md's era through this comparison.
2. **The one commit since compares/3.md (`e28a5e3`) is confirmed
   observationally inert**, checked at the specific point most likely to
   reveal otherwise (the exact seed-10 divergence boundary) rather than only
   at the coarse budgets already known to be insensitive to it.
3. **The `swapChains` mechanism MIGRATION.md documented last session holds up
   under independent re-derivation** — not just re-cited from memory, rebuilt
   and re-run from scratch.
4. Nothing new to report beyond that. When a fix is designed to be inert and
   validated as inert at commit time, the interesting outcome of the next
   comparison is confirming it stayed that way — which is what happened here.
